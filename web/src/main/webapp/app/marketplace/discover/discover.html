How the brokerage works

Four steps, and money only moves after the session happens.

  1. 1 · Search

    Filter by need, format, city, price and rating. Every profile shows credentials and real reviews.

  2. 2 · Request

    Send a request for a specific slot. The professional accepts or proposes another time.

  3. 3 · Meet

    Online, at their studio, or at your home. Carers and home visitors are all background-checked.

  4. 4 · Settle

    The fee is held and released after the session, with the brokerage fee already included in the price you see. The free cancellation window is shown before you confirm.

Scope note. Everyone on this marketplace is a non-medical wellbeing professional. They do not diagnose, prescribe or replace clinical care. Where a need is clinical, the platform routes it back to your BridgeCare care team rather than to a listing.
